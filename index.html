Privacy Policy

The Builders Hive  •  Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains how Omnetics Tech Solutions LLP, operating the service known as The Builders Hive ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information, including lead information collected through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Lead Ads on behalf of our business clients.

1. Who We Are

The Builders Hive is a lead-integration and CRM service operated by Omnetics Tech Solutions LLP, a Limited Liability Partnership registered in India ("we", "us", "our"). We build and operate software that receives leads generated through Meta Lead Ads and delivers them into our clients' customer relationship management (CRM) systems so that our clients can follow up with prospective customers.

For leads collected through a client's Facebook Page and ad campaigns, the client (the business running the ads) is the controller of that personal data, and The Builders Hive acts as a data processor that transfers and stores the data on the client's behalf. For our own website and direct enquiries, The Builders Hive is the controller.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Lead information via Meta Lead Ads

When a person submits an instant form on a Facebook or Instagram lead ad belonging to one of our clients, and that client has authorized our application to access their leads, we receive the information the person provided in that form. This is limited to basic contact information and any qualifying question the client adds to their form:

  • First name and last name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Answers to a qualifying question the client includes on their form, such as property interest
  • Associated identifiers from Meta, such as the lead ID, form ID, page ID, ad ID, and submission timestamp

We only receive the fields that the client's own lead form requested and that the person chose to submit. We do not collect Meta login credentials, and we do not access a person's Facebook or Instagram profile beyond the lead they submitted.

2.2 Information from our website

If you contact us directly or submit a form on our own website, we may collect the name, email, phone number, and message you provide, together with basic technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and diagnostics.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Retrieve new leads from Meta in real time and deliver them into the relevant client's CRM;
  • Enable our clients to view, organize, and follow up with the people who submitted their lead forms;
  • Operate, secure, debug, and improve our lead-integration service;
  • Respond to direct enquiries and provide customer support;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use lead data for our own advertising, and we do not repurpose one client's lead data for any other client.

4. Meta Platform Data

Our use of information obtained through Meta's APIs (including the leads_retrieval permission and the leadgen webhook) complies with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Lead data obtained through Meta is used solely to deliver those leads to the authorizing client's CRM and for no other purpose. Access is granted by the client through Meta's Leads Access and partner permissions and can be revoked by the client at any time.

5. How We Share Information

We share personal information only:

  • With the client who owns the lead ad and lead form that the person submitted;
  • With infrastructure and service providers (such as hosting and database providers) that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations;
  • Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

6. Data Retention

We retain lead data only as long as needed to deliver it to the client's CRM and to provide our service, or for the retention period the client instructs. Once a lead has been delivered and the client relationship or retention period ends, we delete or anonymize the associated lead data within a reasonable period, unless a longer retention is required by law. Clients may request deletion of their leads from our systems at any time.

7. Data Security

We protect personal information using industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls that isolate each client's data, restricted administrative access, and secure storage of access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because the client running the ad is the controller of lead data, requests relating to a specific lead may be directed to that client; we will assist the client in fulfilling such requests. You may also contact us directly using the details below and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your personal information from our systems, email us at the address below with enough detail to identify the relevant record (for example, the email or phone number submitted). We will process verified deletion requests promptly and confirm once complete.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
